Who is behind the code

I'm Adhi, a vocational student in Surakarta. I got into games by modding, then wanted to know how the systems under the mods actually worked. Now I build small, finished games rather than big unfinished ones.

I like movement that feels good, tight levels, and menus that never get in the way.
